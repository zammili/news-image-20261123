An entry for the make.fun bounty **news-image-20261123** — "Sum up
23 November's world news in one image."

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) draws a complete newspaper
front page — masthead, dateline, lead illustration and columns — pixel by
pixel in code and writes it to `/output/entry.png` (1024 × 768, PNG).
No photographs, no stock imagery, no network at run time; the output is
byte-for-byte deterministic across runs.

Run it locally:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

## The story chosen

The lead is the collapse of the COP31 climate summit: **the UN talks in
Antalya, Turkey ended on Sunday without a deal to tackle climate change,
after two days of overtime broke down over fossil fuels and money**. The
EU and about 130 other parties rejected a final text that did not mention
fossil fuels at all, and the talks — due to finish on Friday — ran until
late Sunday, becoming the longest UN climate conference in history. The
illustration shows the sun setting over the Antalya coast, the conference
hall with its flags, and rows of empty chairs.

Around it, the day's other front-page stories:

- **Ukraine** — Ukraine said its naval drones sank the Russian corvette
  Merkury at the Benghazi naval base in Libya; the toll from a Russian
  strike on flats in Dymer, near Kyiv, rose to 11.
- **The Gulf** — President Trump warned Iranian vessels testing the US
  naval blockade would end up "at the bottom of the sea" and signalled
  strikes could resume after the New Year.
- **Markets** — the AI sell-off rattled world markets: Japan's Nikkei
  fell 5.06% and the S&P 500 dropped 3.7%.
- In the ticker: Democrats won 258 House seats, their largest majority
  since 1992; an Indonesia factory fire left 24 dead and about 30
  missing; Bolsonaro in intensive care; Marc Marquez won an eighth
  MotoGP title.

Headline sources relied on: the OneTurn News day digest for
23 November 2026 (COP31 collapse; the Dymer toll; the Nikkei and S&P 500
falls), Just Security's Early Edition for 23 November 2026 (COP31 ending
without a deal; the Bahrain strike and Gulf developments; the Dymer
strike), World Brief Now's 6 AM brief for Monday 23 November 2026
(Ukraine's sea drones sinking the Merkury; the Dymer toll rising to 11;
Trump's blockade warning; the Indonesia factory fire), and Breitbart's
report of President Trump's 22 November Fox News interview (the
"bottom of the sea" warning and strikes after the New Year).
